Quiz 2 - kRR A1
---------------
1. Every student who studies hard passes the exam.
2. Some doctors are researchers.
3. All cats are animals, and some animals are not pets.
4. If a person is a parent, then they have at least one child.
5. No student likes every subject.
6. There exists a teacher who teaches all students.
7. If a number is even, then it is divisible by 2.
8. Some employees work in every department.
9. All birds can fly, except penguins.
10. A person is happy if they have a friend who is honest.


================================================
solution 
================================================



1. Every student who studies hard passes the exam.

FOL:
∀x [(Student(x) ∧ StudiesHard(x)) → PassesExam(x)]

Prolog:
passes_exam(X) :- student(X), studies_hard(X).

2. Some doctors are researchers.

FOL:
∃x [Doctor(x) ∧ Researcher(x)]

Prolog:
doctor(X), researcher(X).
(or a fact like: researcher(john) :- doctor(john). if instantiating)

3. All cats are animals, and some animals are not pets.

FOL:
∀x [Cat(x) → Animal(x)]
∧ ∃x [Animal(x) ∧ ¬Pet(x)]

Prolog:
animal(X) :- cat(X).
animal(X), + pet(X).

4. If a person is a parent, then they have at least one child.

FOL:
∀x [Parent(x) → ∃y Child(y, x)]

Prolog:
has_child(X) :- parent(X), child(Y, X).

5. No student likes every subject.

FOL:
∀x [Student(x) → ¬∀y (Subject(y) → Likes(x, y))]

(equivalently: ∀x [Student(x) → ∃y (Subject(y) ∧ ¬Likes(x,y))])

Prolog:
:- student(X), forall(subject(Y), likes(X,Y)).
(constraint form)

6. There exists a teacher who teaches all students.

FOL:
∃x [Teacher(x) ∧ ∀y (Student(y) → Teaches(x,y))]

Prolog:
teacher(X), forall(student(Y), teaches(X,Y)).

7. If a number is even, then it is divisible by 2.

FOL:
∀x [Even(x) → DivisibleBy(x,2)]

Prolog:
divisible_by(X,2) :- even(X).

8. Some employees work in every department.

FOL:
∃x [Employee(x) ∧ ∀y (Department(y) → WorksIn(x,y))]

Prolog:
employee(X), forall(department(Y), works_in(X,Y)).

9. All birds can fly, except penguins.

FOL:
∀x [(Bird(x) ∧ ¬Penguin(x)) → CanFly(x)]
∧ ∀x [Penguin(x) → Bird(x)]

Prolog:
can_fly(X) :- bird(X), + penguin(X).
bird(X) :- penguin(X).

10. A person is happy if they have a friend who is honest.

FOL:
∀x [(Person(x) ∧ ∃y (Friend(x,y) ∧ Honest(y))) → Happy(x)]

Prolog:
happy(X) :- person(X), friend(X,Y), honest(Y).
